Canola trial: Confidence Interval Chart

Choose a measurement and 2 or 3 treatments. There is a 95% confidence interval being used for each treatment.

Checks

    Chart

    Every diamond in the interval represents an average for treatments. The bar is the 95% confidence interval: the range where the true average probably falls.

    The numbers

    Formula for intervals:

    mean ± t × (SD ÷ √n)

    Here n = 6 (3 replicates × 2 years), so t = 2.571 for 95% confidence.

    What does it mean?

    Rule of thumb: Should two intervals not overlap then treatments probably really differ. If they overlap, we cannot be sure. This rule is cautious, so it can miss some real differences.(Rule is not 100% accurate)
